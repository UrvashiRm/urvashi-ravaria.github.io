Alchemist Marketing: Real Estate Campaign Direction

Mainline Marketing Intern at Alchemist, working across two real-estate clients at opposite ends of the market — Runwal Enterprises and JP Infra — translating client positioning into creative direction the design team could execute.

Creative Direction Client Servicing OOH / Print Real Estate

The challenge

JP Parkway needed to shift buyer perception from "affordable housing" toward "premium lifestyle" ahead of launch. My job was translating that positioning brief into creative direction the design team could execute, and holding the tone across every asset in the campaign.

My role

  • Sat in client meetings and site visits to understand what JP Infra wanted the brand to feel like
  • Converted client input into creative briefs for the design team
  • Coordinated with the design team through 3 rounds of revision to hit the right brand tone
  • Compiled and delivered monthly retainer reports the client used to track output

The range

Runwal Garden City sits at the opposite end of the market from JP Parkway — 1 & 2 BHKs starting ₹48L against JP Parkway's premium ₹99L positioning. Same agency, same intern, two different creative voices, built for two different buyers. That adaptation is the actual skill this work required.

Output across both clients: 40+ OOH and print executions, plus 10+ mailer creatives a month for festive-season campaigns. Creative direction developed in collaboration with Alchemist's in-house design team.
