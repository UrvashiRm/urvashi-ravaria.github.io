Winning first place in SP Jain's competitive business pitch showcase validated the strategic approach to market entry and go-to-market sequencing before entering contested pharmaceutical space. --- ## Why I Built This The UAE cold-chain market is growing 7–8% annually (USD 1.4–2.9B by 2033), driven by high food import dependence and strict regulatory requirements. Dubai Municipality mandates HACCP-based temperature records for all high-risk foods, yet operators still use disconnected data loggers, manual clipboards, and fragmented spreadsheets. A single temperature excursion spoils the product and destroys the audit trail simultaneously — operators lose both the cargo value and compliance proof in one event. ColdProof solves this by centralizing monitoring, alerting operators before a breach occurs, and generating audit-ready compliance reports on demand. --- ## The Strategic Play **Why food before pharma:** - Food operators run 150–250 monitored points; faster buying decisions; moderate willingness to pay - Global pharma-grade incumbents (Sensitech, ELPRO, Controlant) over-serve high-value pharmaceutical routes and under-price the mid-market food segment - Beachhead proves the model; later pivot upmarket into pharmaceutical with proof of adoption **Why land-and-expand sequencing:** - Land: Direct B2B into one live facility; prove alerts work; build reference site - Expand: Same operator adds rooms/vehicles over time; per-point pricing creates natural growth - Scale: Channel partnerships (sensor suppliers, auditors, insurers) only after food-side validation **Why this positioning is defensible:** - Regulatory lock-in: operators won't remove the system generating audit evidence before an inspection - Data moat: accumulated excursion history makes the system more valuable as it runs - Regulatory mapping: compliance templates customized per emirate reduce switching cost --- ## The Numbers | Metric | Value | |--------|-------| | Mid-size operator | 200 monitored points | | Price per point/month | AED 40 (mid-point of AED 30–80 range) | | Monthly recurring revenue/account | AED 8,000 | | Annual recurring revenue/account | AED 96,000 | | Gross margin | 70–80% | | Build cost (v1) | AED 0.8–1.5M | | 10-account revenue run rate | AED 4.8M+/year | **What these numbers mean:** Asset-light model with recurring revenue eliminates the capital burden of owning refrigeration. Gross margin stays high because hardware is passed through to customers or amortized; defensible asset is the compliance record. --- ## Validated vs. Unproven **What I validated:** - Market need is real: regulatory mandate + food loss problem documented in peer-reviewed literature - Competitor gap exists: incumbents neglect the mid-market food segment - Technology stack is proven: LoRaWAN, Bluetooth, cloud infrastructure, SaaS billing all mature **What's still unproven:** - Willingness to pay in food segment (assumed moderate; needs pilot deployment) - Sales cycle velocity (unknown until first live facility is onboarded) **How I'd test it:** - Pilot with one cold-storage operator: measure alert reliability, adoption inside facility, CAC payback - Instrument from day one: track ARR, net revenue retention, gross margin against projections - Results determine next phase funding and channel expansion sequence --- ## Downloads [Download Full Business Plan (PDF) ↗](./coldproof-business-plan.pdf) [Download 5-Slide Pitch Deck (PDF) ↗](./coldproof-pitch-deck.pdf) --- ## Context This work was completed as part of SP Jain's Entrepreneurship for a Disruptive Launch module, taught by Prof. Roshni Paul.
