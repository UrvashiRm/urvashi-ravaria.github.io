Cold-chain compliance platform for UAE food operators

Why I built this

The UAE cold-chain market is growing 7–8% annually, projected to reach USD 1.4–2.9B by 2033, driven by high food import dependence and strict regulatory requirements. Dubai Municipality mandates HACCP-based temperature records for all high-risk foods, yet most operators still run on disconnected data loggers, manual clipboards, and fragmented spreadsheets. A single temperature excursion spoils the product and destroys the audit trail at the same time — operators lose the cargo value and the compliance proof in one event. ColdProof centralises monitoring, alerts operators before a breach happens, and generates audit-ready compliance reports on demand.

The strategic play

Why food before pharma

  • Food operators run 150–250 monitored points, move faster on buying decisions, and show moderate willingness to pay
  • Global pharma-grade incumbents (Sensitech, ELPRO, Controlant) over-serve high-value pharmaceutical routes and under-price the mid-market food segment
  • The food beachhead proves the model before a later pivot upmarket into pharmaceutical, backed by proof of adoption

Why land-and-expand sequencing

  • Land: direct B2B into one live facility, prove the alerts work, build a reference site
  • Expand: same operator adds rooms and vehicles over time, per-point pricing creates natural growth
  • Scale: channel partnerships with sensor suppliers, auditors, and insurers, only after food-side validation

Why this positioning is defensible

  • Regulatory lock-in — operators won't remove the system generating their audit evidence before an inspection
  • A data moat — accumulated excursion history makes the system more valuable the longer it runs
  • Regulatory mapping — compliance templates customised per emirate raise the switching cost

This is an asset-light model — recurring revenue without the capital burden of owning refrigeration. Gross margin stays high because hardware is passed through to customers or amortised; the defensible asset is the compliance record itself, not the equipment.

Validated vs. unproven

Validated

  • Market need is real — the regulatory mandate and food-loss problem are documented in peer-reviewed literature
  • A competitor gap exists — incumbents neglect the mid-market food segment
  • The technology stack is proven — LoRaWAN, Bluetooth, cloud infrastructure, and SaaS billing are all mature

Still unproven

  • Willingness to pay in the food segment — assumed moderate, needs a pilot deployment
  • Sales cycle velocity — unknown until the first live facility is onboarded

How I'd test it: run a pilot with one cold-storage operator and measure alert reliability, in-facility adoption, and CAC payback. Instrument from day one — track ARR, net revenue retention, and gross margin against projections — and let the results decide the next funding phase and channel sequence.
